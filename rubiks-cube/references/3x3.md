# 3x3 reference

All algs here are checked by `scripts/test_cube.py`. "Case" patterns describe
the U face: yellow (or whatever is on top) is the last layer.

## Beginner method (layer by layer)

1. **Cross** on the bottom (white). Intuitive. Match edge side colours to centers.
2. **First-layer corners.** Put the corner above its slot (front-right, top layer)
   and repeat `R U R' U'` until it is in place and white faces down.
3. **Second-layer edges.** Find a top edge without yellow. Turn U so its front
   colour matches the front center. Then:
   - edge goes to the right: `U R U' R' U' F' U F`
   - edge goes to the left: `U' L' U L U F U' F'`
   An edge stuck in the middle layer the wrong way: insert any top edge into
   that slot to push it out, then insert it properly.
4. **Yellow cross** (edge orientation). Look at the yellow edges on top:
   - dot (no edges): do the line alg, then the L alg (or `F R U R' U' F' Fw R U R' U' Fw'`)
   - L shape at back-left (yellow at UB and UL): `F U R U' R' F'`
   - L shape at front-right (yellow at UF and UR): `Fw R U R' U' Fw'`
   - line going left-right: `F R U R' U' F'`
5. **Position yellow corners.** `U R U' L' U R' U' L` cycles the three corners
   UFL → UBR → UBL (it keeps UFR where it is). Find one corner already in the right
   spot, hold it at front-right, repeat until all four are placed.
6. **Twist yellow corners.** Hold the cube so a wrong corner is at UFR. Do
   `R' D' R D` 2 or 4 times until that corner is right. The bottom looks broken,
   don't panic. Turn **only U** to bring the next wrong corner to UFR, repeat.
   When all are done the bottom fixes itself.
7. **Position yellow edges.** Use Ua/Ub (below). If no edge is right, do either once first.

## 2-look OLL (orient last layer)

Step 1: edges (see step 4 above). Step 2: corners, with the yellow cross done.
Pattern = which corners already show yellow on top.

| Case | Hold | Alg |
|---|---|---|
| Sune: 1 corner done | done corner front-left | `R U R' U R U2 R'` |
| Antisune: 1 corner done | done corner back-right | `R U2 R' U' R U' R'` |
| H: 0 done, two yellow stickers on the left side and two on the right | yellow pairs facing left and right | `R U R' U R U' R' U R U2 R'` |
| Pi: 0 done, not H | yellow pair facing left | `R U2 R2 U' R2 U' R2 U2 R` |
| Headlights: 2 adjacent done | done corners at the back | `R2 D R' U2 R D' R' U2 R'` |
| T / chameleon: 2 adjacent done | done corners on the right | `Rw U R' U' Rw' F R F'` |
| Bowtie: 2 diagonal done | done corners back-right and front-left | `F' Rw U R' U' Rw' F R` |

H vs Pi: with 0 corners done, look at the side stickers. H has two yellow pairs
on opposite sides. Pi has one yellow pair on one side, and the other two yellow
stickers face front and back. If unsure of a hold, show the user the output of
`cube.py case "ALG"`: it prints the exact state the alg solves.

## 2-look PLL (permute last layer)

Step 1: corners. Look for "headlights" (two corners with the same colour on one side).

| Case | Alg | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| headlights on one side: T-perm (hold headlights on the left) | `R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'` | swaps UFR↔UBR, UL↔UR |
| no headlights: Y-perm | `F R U' R' U' R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R F'` | swaps UFR↔UBL, UL↔UB |
| headlights on all sides | corners are done | |

Step 2: edges.

| Case | Alg | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Ua (solved side at back) | `R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2` | UF → UR → UL → UF |
| Ub (solved side at back) | `R2 U R U R' U' R' U' R' U R'` | UF → UL → UR → UF |
| H: swap opposite edges | `M2 U M2 U2 M2 U M2` | UF↔UB, UL↔UR |
| Z: swap adjacent pairs | `M' U M2 U M2 U M' U2 M2 U'` | UF↔UL, UB↔UR |

Extra, useful: Aa `x R' U R' D2 R U' R' D2 R2 x'` (corners UBL → UBR → UFR),
Ab `x R2 D2 R U R' D2 R U' R x'` (other direction). Both keep UFL.

## Fixing single things

- **Flip two edges in place** (UF and UB, nothing else): `M' U M' U M' U2 M U M U M U2`.
  For two other edges: bring them to UF and UB with setup moves, do the alg,
  undo the setup moves.
- **Twist two corners in place**: hold one at UFR, do `R' D' R D` until right,
  turn U to bring the other to UFR, repeat the same count the other way. (Step 6 method.)
- **Swap two corners and two edges**: T-perm or Y-perm (with setup moves).

## Impossible states on a 3x3

A real 3x3 can never have:

- exactly **one edge flipped** (edges flip in pairs),
- exactly **one corner twisted** (twists sum to zero),
- exactly **two pieces swapped** with nothing else changed (swaps come in pairs:
  two corners swapped means two edges are swapped too).

If a user describes one of these, the cube was taken apart and put back wrong,
or a sticker was moved. Fix: take out the piece and put it back correctly.
Do not search for an alg; none exists. (On a 4x4 these can happen: see `4x4.md`.)
